# Roadmap

This roadmap represents the intended direction of Bisync Hub.

The roadmap may evolve as the project grows.

---

# Vision

Become the safest and most understandable way to operate Rclone Bisync.

Review. Decide. Sync.

---

# Near-Term Priorities

## Test Coverage Expansion

Goals:

- >80% overall coverage
- Service coverage improvements
- API coverage improvements
- End-to-end workflow testing

---

## OAuth Reconnect

Allow users to re-authenticate remotes directly from the dashboard.

Benefits:

- Faster recovery
- Reduced CLI dependency
- Better usability

---

# Interactive Sync Review

Primary strategic feature.

Capabilities:

- Deletion review
- Conflict review
- Per-file decisions
- Action previews
- Rollback support
- Crash recovery

---

# Multi-Profile Synchronization

Support multiple synchronization profiles from a single dashboard.

Examples:

- Personal Google Drive
- Business OneDrive
- NAS synchronization

---

# Configuration Management

Provide safe editing of:

- Sync settings
- Performance settings
- Notifications

with validation and rollback capabilities.

---

# Hardening

Future improvements:

- Secure credential generation
- Unix socket transport
- Permission consistency improvements

---

# Long-Term Direction

Bisync Hub is not intended to become:

- A cloud service
- A hosted SaaS platform
- A replacement for Rclone

Its purpose is to improve the user experience around Rclone Bisync.

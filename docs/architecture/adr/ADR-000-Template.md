# ADR-000: Architecture Decision Record Template

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-10-26

---

## Context

What problem are we solving?

Briefly describe:

- The operational, product, or architectural issue.
- Technical constraints and environmental assumptions.
- Why an explicit decision is needed now.

---

## Decision

State the decision clearly and unambiguously.

Describe:

- What architectural pattern, technology, or convention was chosen.
- What contributors, maintainers, and automated agents should assume going forward.
- Boundaries of what is *in* and *out* of scope.

---

## Rationale

Why was this decision made?

- What alternatives were evaluated?
- What are the key technical and product arguments favoring this approach?
- Why were other options discarded?

---

## Consequences

### Positive
- Specific architectural benefits (e.g., safety, testability, performance, maintainability).
- Developer experience improvements.

### Negative / Trade-offs
- Added complexity, constraints, or migrations required.
- Operational overhead or limitations introduced.
